# Audio

Ten short original synthesized cues created for this demo (2026-11-03): place, warn, hit, hurt, counter, start, win, lose, spring, phase.

Generated with deterministic sine/square oscillators and amplitude envelopes, 22050 Hz mono PCM WAV. No external recordings, samples, or third-party audio assets. Mixed at -12 dB with bounded polyphony. M or the speaker button toggles effects.
